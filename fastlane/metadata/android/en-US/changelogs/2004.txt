Trellis 1.0 is a rebuild: everything the old app did, plus the whole intake side.

• Read anything — EPUB, articles, pasted text, Gutenberg — four ways
• Podcasts and RSS; episodes transcribe on your own device, in their own language
• Courses and backups import as before; the study engine is unchanged
• Encrypted backup, Anki export, household dashboard

The app now asks for network permission: the feeds you add, the models you choose. Still no accounts, no ads, no tracking.
