Read, listen, study, remember — one local-first app for a household's whole knowledge diet.

Trellis is a speed-reader, a podcast player that transcribes episodes on your own device, and a study engine that drills what you keep into durable memory.

• Read four ways: RSVP speed-reading, plain print, a ticker, or spoken aloud — your place is one position that survives switching between them
• Bring anything in: EPUB, web articles, pasted text, Project Gutenberg, RSS and podcast feeds
• Transcribe podcasts on the device, in the language they are spoken — including an English layer via Whisper's translate task
• Latest lists every episode and article from your feeds, newest first, and keeps them all: no ranking, no streaks, no engagement tricks
• A Queue tab holds what plays next, in the order you choose, and only what you put there
• Study concepts on a prerequisite lattice: a concept stays locked until the ones it builds on hold
• Four kinds of recall item, scheduled by spaced repetition with a monotonic-interval guarantee
• Export your cards to Anki whenever you want
• Encrypted backup with a key only you hold — restores 0.x Trellis and ohPrimer backups too
• Optional: bring your own API key to distill sources into courses; off by default, never required

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The network is used only for the feeds you add and the models you choose to download.

Free and open source, MIT licensed.
