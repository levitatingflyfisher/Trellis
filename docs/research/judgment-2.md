## Rationale
Espalier-Flutter wins on the values lens, with a points tie against Arbor broken three ways. (1) Privacy is structural only in P2: I verified the donor's proxy chain (cors.eu.org/allorigins/codetabs) and that WebView shells don't escape CORS — so P1, P3, and hearth-less P4 all leak reading/listening habits to consent-gated third parties on the family's primary surface, while P2's native fetch removes the proxy layer entirely rather than fencing it. (2) The canonical podcast-language-learner's GUARANTEED path is best in P2: direct feed fetch → checkpointed foreground-service native transcription that survives screen-off (multilingual whisper.cpp + translate task, fixing the verified whisper-tiny.en English-only defect) → word-timed ledger cards → typed study, all on one spine in one app; Arbor's equally good story is conditional on a hearth daemon plus a research-grade WebTransport channel, and its guaranteed path is WASM-in-a-WebView with the screen on. (3) Genuine craft for one artisan: P2 consumes the fleet's four hardest-won Dart assets as direct deps — I verified the Trellis study domain is pure Dart with zero Flutter imports, so the 183-test engine (including both crown-jewel property tests) lands unmodified — plus sanctuary_auth_core's OHBK, domovoi's ResumableTransfer/stove client (verified present), and the C1–C7 conformance harness; every other proposal re-implements frozen wire formats and a tested scheduler in a second language and maintains the drift forever. HYBRID VERDICT (explicit adoptions into P2): (a) steal P4's .ohparcel "cuttings" — a course/parcel carrying transcript + alignment + translation layers — as the potato-floor and iOS delivery vehicle; it is the single best idea on the panel and P2's .ohcourse pipeline can carry it; (b) steal P1's distill-time discourse-baked-into-the-course move so T0 devices get construction-and-discourse study with zero runtime inference; (c) steal P4's revlog-as-deterministic-fold as the sync-ready form of P2's revlog, and adopt Rootstock as the ROADMAP for P2's desktop surface (the same Flutter desktop app grows overnight jobs and stove serving — P4's household vision on P2's chassis, honoring the owner's "server nobody has to trust" framing); (d) pull opus-mt/onnx seq2seq forward from Phase 6 if the canonical user's sentence-MT gap bites, since v1's Brain-gated translation is P2's weakest end-to-end link.

## Red flags
- P3 (fatal): resurrects the two-app ADR-0006 shape the fleet already tried and killed (ADR-0008 made Trellis the sole reader after the split failed; direction has flipped twice already) — and it violates the 'do not lose any functionality, only gain' commission by design: review-where-you-read is dropped, mnemonic-medium reading is structurally impossible in Grist (no scheduler there), and podcast courses reach Trellis with their audio layers ignored. The seam corpus is excellent engineering applied to re-opening a healed wound.
- P3: adds the INTERNET permission to shipped, zero-network Trellis — a values-surface regression on the fleet's cleanest artifact, honestly fenced but real.
- P1 (near-fatal, flagged by the proposal itself): the in-place install-base upgrade (com.openhearth.trellis, versionCode 4) replaces the Flutter app with a WebView shell whose PWA cannot read SharedPreferences — existing users lose SRS state unless the 'optional' prefs bridge ships; against the fleet's no-backwards-compat-theater law this must be decided and tested BEFORE any APK ships, not left a Phase-F nicety.
- P1/P3/P4-client: podcast enclosure bytes for transcription route through public CORS proxies (donor caps 300MB/audio through allorigins-class third parties) on the primary phone surface — consent-gated is not the same as structural; the values lens's first question is answered 'a third party, if you consent' on three of four proposals.
- P4: the browser↔hearth channel (WebTransport + serverCertificateHashes over a maintained Node HTTP/3 stack, Safari unproven) is research-grade; if it slips, all hearth value concentrates in the WebView APK — which embeds a Dart stove client, i.e. quietly rebuilds part of proposal 2's native layer anyway. Also invents a second AEAD protocol plus HLC/LWW sync; StillLife's sync-clock bug history (20 concurrent stamps→1) is the cautionary precedent the proposal itself cites.
- P4: largest scope on the panel (15–18 focused weeks, four surfaces plus a daemon with Node-SEA-plus-native-deps packaging) for one artisan; its own risk list names P1-phase overrun as likeliest — the v0.5 client-only hedge is real but the household promise is the pitch, and it may arrive months late or never.
- P2: v1.0 web surface ships zero local ML (a regression vs the donor's janky-but-present browser Whisper) and drops Tesseract OCR outright — two edges where 'feature superset' is not literally met; the honesty screen and Phase-6 commitment must ship, and the OCR drop should be reconsidered (household-desktop OCR job is the obvious cure).
- P2: v1 translation is Brain-gated (opus-mt/NLLB native seq2seq deferred to Phase 6) — a potato user with no Brain tier gets only Whisper's X→English; the 200-language claim is not redeemable offline at launch. The FFI build matrix (whisper/llama/piper/onnxruntime × ABIs) plus the retired-upstream ffmpeg_kit fork is real permanent solo maintenance.
- All four: multilingual word-level timestamps ride documented-fragile paths (broken _timestamped exports, fp16 encoder bug, non-English drift) — every proposal correctly promises sentence-level as the contract; any marketing of word-level karaoke beyond English is overclaim.
- All four PWAs: Safari/iOS 7-day eviction can vaporize a library unless install-to-home-screen is actively pushed; only P2's native APK is structurally immune on the primary surface (and no proposal ships native iOS at all — the iOS family member's tier should be stated plainly in whichever VISION.md gets written).
