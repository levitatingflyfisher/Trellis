## Rationale
P1 (Espalier-TS) wins on the craft lens, by a nose over P2, on one decisive asymmetry plus the lens's own burden checklist — with an explicit hybrid mandate below.

**The rewrite-mass asymmetry (verified on disk).** Donor A's GOOD layer is its reader/intake/comms — 8065 lines of proven, heuristic-laden behavior whose in-language port is already underway (rebuild/ exists with 7 extracted modules and a working verbatim-extraction + regression-ID method). Donor A's JANKY layer was ML. P1 ports the big good layer in-language and rewrites only the small, exhaustively-specified layers: the study engine (pure functions with a ~180-test matrix as its spec), OHBK (a frozen wire format with a yellow paper — the entire point of a yellow paper is to enable a second implementation), and the domovoi download laws (documented as portable laws in the source). Each rewrite is verifiable against cross-implementation goldens generated by the surviving Dart reference. P2 inverts this: it gets the study engine free (its trump card is real — I confirmed zero Flutter imports in the study domain) but must rewrite the largest, least-specified donor layer in a new language while simultaneously standing up an FFI build matrix. Craft law: rewrite the janky layer on better foundations, port the good layer verbatim. P1 obeys it; P2 violates it.

**The lens's burden checklist.** Two codebases? FFI builds per platform? Server ops? P1 pays none of the three (the WebView shell is Parlour-proven near-zero maintenance). P2 pays FFI-per-platform forever — and this specific machine has gcc but no cmake and no clang, friction its risk list doesn't name. P3 pays three repos. P4 pays daemon ops plus a sync protocol.

**The T0 hard requirement says "cheap Android BROWSER."** A ≤300KB-gz TS shell on a 2GB phone is a different physics class from a Flutter-web canvaskit boot (the fleet's own deploy playbook documents the slow-boot-spinner mitigation). P1/P4's DOM typography also gives the highest beauty ceiling for a print-like reading app; the reader — the heart of the product — ports with its look intact.

**Velocity.** Same-language porting of the largest layer + an already-proven porting method + the smallest total rewrite mass makes P1's ~9-week first-delicious the most credible claim in the packet. The workshop's web competence is proven by donor A itself.

**What P2 gets right that P1 must adopt (the hybrid):** (1) The phone is where the commissioning pain lives — "failed transcripts, transcripts that took ages" — and native whisper.cpp + a foreground service is the strongest answer. P1 must pull its Tauri/native-sidecar phase forward from "post-1.0" to immediately-after-v0.1, and its Android shell must NOT stay dumb: it needs a MediaSession/foreground-service audio bridge so car-podcast playback survives screen-off (see red flags). (2) P2's blunt stove-transport honesty (browser stove is infeasible today; OllamaBrain day one, StoveBrain staged) is the right sequencing — P1 already mirrors it; keep it. (3) System-TTS-at-T0 — P1 has the Web Speech equivalent; keep it load-bearing.

**Steal from the losers:** P3's conformance-corpus-as-referee is the best testing idea in the packet — adopt it as an in-repo `corpus/` for .ohcourse (valid/invalid/v1-forever/update-pairs) that both the TS parser CI and any future reader must pass, without the standalone spec repo. P4's revlog-fold (scheduler state as a deterministic fold over an append-only log, property-tested) is the cleanest possible FSRS-readiness story — adopt it for P1's revlog. P4's .ohparcel cutting (transcript+alignment+translation traveling as data to zero-ML devices) is the fullest version of the potato-floor insight; P1's discourse-baked-into-courses is the same idea — extend it to full parcels.

**Why not P2 outright:** its v1.0 web surface ships with zero local ML — a capability donor A had (jankily, English-only, but real), which breaches the letter of hard requirement 1 on one surface and collides with the owner's documented don't-defer/don't-stage law. Why not P4: the client-only v0.5 is essentially P1-lite, and everything beyond it (daemon packaging, sync, WebTransport) is the highest standing complexity one artisan can sign up for. Why not P3: ADR-0006's own supersession note names "two codebases to keep format-compatible" as exactly what forced the retirement; P3's corpus mechanism is a genuinely good answer to that cost, but re-opening a twice-flipped decision while regressing review-where-you-read to an app switch fails the craft test of one coherent product.

## Red flags
- P1 (fatal if unaddressed): car-podcast playback in a dumb WebView shell — background audio with screen off and lock-screen controls is unsolved in the proposal; the canonical user listens in the car, and a WebView inherits browser lifecycle (process kill, no MediaSession by default). The shell must grow a foreground-service media bridge (no longer 'one main.dart'), or the flagship story is fragile on the primary mobile surface.
- P1: the in-place APK upgrade (com.openhearth.trellis, versionCode 4) replaces a native Flutter app with a WebView shell and creates a data cliff for anyone who never exported .ohbk — the proposal's own risks admit the prefs-bridge is optional; per the house no-backwards-compat-theater law a clean break is allowed, but this must be DECIDED, not drifted into, before the APK ships.
- P1: COOP/COEP-via-service-worker is a hack with a first-visit reload; if browsers tighten it, T1 potato transcription drops to single-threaded WASM — the exact 'took ages' jank the rebuild was commissioned to kill.
- P2: v1.0 web surface has zero local ML where donor A had working (English) in-browser transcription — a hard-requirement-1 breach on one surface, staged to Phase 6, colliding with the owner's documented don't-defer law.
- P2: the FFI build matrix (whisper.cpp/llama.cpp/Piper/onnxruntime × Android arm64+armv7 × 3 desktops) is named by the craft lens itself as the burden to fear; verified: this box has gcc but no cmake and no clang, friction the proposal's risk list omits.
- P3: re-litigates a twice-flipped decision — ADR-0006's supersession note (verified on disk) names 'two codebases to keep format-compatible' as exactly what forced ohPrimer's retirement; the corpus/validate-CLI reduces but does not zero the coordination tax, and 'discipline is still a dependency' is the proposal's own admission.
- P3: review-where-you-read regresses from same-tab (donor A) to an app-switch on Android and a file dance on iOS/desktop — the graded study loop, the product's core, acquires permanent friction; and Trellis gains the INTERNET permission, surrendering its grep-clean zero-network status.
- P4: highest standing complexity in the packet for one artisan — four surfaces plus a sync protocol (HLC/LWW edge cases; StillLife's 20-concurrent-stamps→1 sync bug is the in-house precedent) plus Node SEA packaging with native deps per OS plus a WebTransport bet Safari may never honor.
- All four: word-level multilingual audio↔text timing rides documented-fragile upstream exports (broken turbo timestamps #1357, fp16 encoder bug #1590, non-English drift) — every proposal correctly demotes the contract to sentence-grade; the marketing and the UI copy must not promise word-grade karaoke for the flagship language-learner case.
- All four proposals' first-delicious estimates (8-14 weeks) carry self-admitted ±40% variance concentrated in the same phase (checkpointed multilingual transcription + downloads); whichever wins, that phase should be built first-thing-after-the-ported-core, not last, so the highest-variance work has the longest runway.
